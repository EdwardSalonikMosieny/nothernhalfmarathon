Our Story

Across the vast landscapes of Northern Kenya, dusty trails cross ancient community lands, wildlife moves through open plains, and resilience is woven into everyday life. From this powerful setting, a race with a deeper purpose was born.

The Northern Kenya Half Marathon is more than a finish line. It is a story of hope, identity, transformation, and untapped greatness rising from one of Africa's most extraordinary regions.

Founded by passionate young visionaries under the leadership of Ruso Jacob through Northern Empowerment Races CBO, the marathon exists to unlock athletic talent, empower communities, inspire youth and women, and build a lasting running culture across Northern Kenya.

For generations, the region has produced strong, naturally gifted runners. Yet many promising young athletes have lacked exposure, training pathways, equipment, mentorship, and opportunities that could transform their futures. This marathon was established to change that story.

"Every stride tells a story - of talent, land, culture, courage, and hope."

What We Do

The Northern Kenya Half Marathon is more than a race. We use athletics as a powerful tool to create opportunities, inspire communities, and drive long-term social impact across Northern Kenya.

Athletic Talent Development

We identify, nurture, and support emerging athletic talent from remote and underserved communities through training, mentorship, exposure, and competition.

Youth Empowerment

We empower young people through sports, leadership, mentorship, discipline, and community engagement on and off the track.

Community Wellness

We promote active lifestyles, fitness, mental well-being, and healthy living through running and recreational sports.

Conservation Awareness

We use sport to promote environmental conservation, land restoration, and sustainable coexistence between communities, wildlife, and nature.

Women & Inclusion

We create inclusive opportunities for women and marginalized groups to participate, lead, compete, and benefit from sports and community programs.

Sports Tourism Promotion

We showcase Northern Kenya's beauty, culture, landscapes, and potential through sporting experiences that attract local and international visitors.

Education & Mentorship

Through sports programs and partnerships, we inspire young people to pursue education, personal growth, leadership, and positive community impact.

Building a Running Culture

We organize races, training sessions, and community fitness initiatives that bring people together through sport.

Community Transformation

Every event and initiative is designed to strengthen communities, create opportunities, and inspire hope for future generations.

Our Mission

To transform lives and communities in Northern Kenya through athletics, empowerment, conservation, and opportunity.

Our Vision

To build Northern Kenya into a globally recognized hub for talent, community impact, conservation, and sports excellence.

The marathon supports conservation awareness, tourism promotion, community empowerment, and international visibility for remote communities that are often overlooked.
